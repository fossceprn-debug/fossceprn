# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in FOSS CEPrn a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

We welcome contributions from people of all backgrounds, experience levels, and identities.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Welcoming different opinions and perspectives.
- Giving constructive feedback.
- Helping newcomers learn and contribute.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Insulting or offensive comments.
- Personal attacks or trolling.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any conduct that creates an unsafe or hostile environment.

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior.

They may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community interactions related to FOSS CEPrn.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainers privately.

All reports will be reviewed and handled appropriately.

## Community

We encourage everyone to contribute positively, respectfully, and collaboratively.

Thank you for helping make FOSS CEPrn a friendly open-source community!

**FOSS CEPrn — Learn. Build. Share. Contribute.**
